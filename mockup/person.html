Biografi

Johanna Maria Lind (1820–1887), known as the "Swedish Nightingale", was one of the most celebrated opera singers of the nineteenth century. She was closely acquainted with H.C. Andersen, who is believed to have harboured romantic feelings for her; she does not appear to have reciprocated. Andersen mentions her repeatedly in his diaries across three decades, most notably in connection with her London triumph in 1847 and her retirement from the operatic stage in the 1850s.
