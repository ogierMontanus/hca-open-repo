Works
In this language

Works whose title is written in the language — a different signal from the two lists below. A German-language work can easily have a Danish author (and vice versa), so these are deliberately kept separate from "by artists from this nation" and never merged with them. The language is derived from the title, not recorded by the editors: works marked register are ones the register itself stated as translations, while derived is machine-determined — see scripts/build_mockup/detect_work_language.py.

By artists from this nation
Set in this nation
How the three lists are found. "In this language" comes from detect_work_language.py, which only tags a title when it is long enough and the determination is confident enough — 1,250 of 3,708 works are tagged, the rest are deliberately left untagged rather than guessed wrong. "By artists" matches the work's author field against the persons above (the same name resolution work.html uses). "Set in" only matches works whose title has a parenthetical place mention that itself exists in the Place Register (e.g. »The Medici Venus (Uffizi, Florence)«). A work can appear in more than one of the lists — that's precisely the point of keeping them separate.
